This website explores how Rick Riordan's Percy Jackson series connects to real ancient Greek and Roman mythology.


What is the Percy Jackson series?

The Percy Jackson series is a collection of fantasy novels written by Rick Riordan. The story follows the adventures of Percy Jackson, a demigod and son of Poseidon, as he navigates the world of Greek mythology in modern times.


What is Greek and Roman mythology?

Greek and Roman mythology refers to the collection of myths, legends, and stories that were part of the religious beliefs of ancient Greece and Rome. These myths often involve gods, goddesses, heroes, and mythical creatures.
