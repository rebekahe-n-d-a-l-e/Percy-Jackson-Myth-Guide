Character Profiles

Percy Jackson

Percy Jackson is a fictional Greek demigod, the son of Poseidon and Sally Jackson, and the main hero of Rick Riordan's Percy Jackson & the Olympians books. He has messy black hair, sea-green eyes, and fights with a magical sword-pen named Riptide. Percy can control water, talk to horses, and is fiercely loyal to his friends, despite his fatal flaw of risking the world to save them.


Annabeth Chase

Daughter of Athena. She is a brilliant strategist, architect, and fierce fighter who uses a celestial bronze dagger and a cap of invisibility. She has blonde hair, gray eyes, and is known for her intelligence, bravery, and loyalty to her friends. Annabeth's fatal flaw is her pride, which can sometimes cloud her judgment.

Grover Underwood

Grover Underwood is a satyr—half-man, half-goat—who serves as Percy Jackson’s anxious yet deeply courageous best friend and protector. Recognizable by his curly hair, small horns hidden under a beanie, and goat legs concealed by fake feet, Grover possesses a powerful sense of smell to track monsters and plays magical reed pipes that control plant life. Though easily frightened and famously obsessed with eating tin cans and cheese enchiladas, he displays incredible bravery in the face of danger, particularly when defending nature and his friends. Over the course of the series, Grover evolves from a clumsy, insecure guide into a respected leader who achieves his ultimate dream of finding the lost wild god, Pan, and inherits a portion of the god's spirit to become an official Lord of the Wild.

Luke Castellan

Luke Castellan is a former friend and later rival of Percy Jackson. He is a demigod son of Hermes and was initially portrayed as a helpful ally, but later revealed to be working against the heroes. He has cropped, sandy-blonde hair, bright blue eyes, and a sharp, charismatic face. Luke is known for his charisma, strategic mind, and ability to manipulate others. His fatal flaw is his desire for power and recognition.
