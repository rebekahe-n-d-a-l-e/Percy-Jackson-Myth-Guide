Gods Comparison

Name of God Domain & Power Relationship with Percy Demigod Offspring Personality Mythology vs. The Book
Poseidon Seas, earthquakes, storms, horses. Father & Protector; supportive but bound by divine laws. Percy Jackson, Tyson (Cyclops). Easygoing, fiercely loyal, powerful when angered. Dressed in Hawaiian shirts and shorts, defying ancient rigidness.
Zeus Sky, lightning, law, justice. Paranoid Rival; views Percy as a rule-breaker and threat. Thalia Grace (daughter of Zeus). Arrogant, dramatic, obsessed with control. Trades robes for sharp business suits; hyper-focused on corporate-style power.
Hades Underworld, riches, death. Unpredictable; has a complex relationship with Percy. Nico di Angelo (son of Hades). Intense, brooding, protective of his family. Often depicted in a dark, somber manner; more human-like in appearance.
Athena Wisdom, warfare, crafts. Mentor and guide; supports Percy's growth and development. Annabeth Chase (daughter of Athena). Clever, strategic, values knowledge and learning. Often portrayed as a wise, mature figure with a strong sense of justice rather than just a warrior.
