Exploring the First Book in the Percy Jackson Series

Quick Short Summary
Twelve-year-old Percy Jackson discovers he isn't just a regular kid, he’s the demigod son of Poseidon, the Greek God of the Sea. When Zeus’s master lightning bolt is stolen, Percy is accused of the crime. He must embark on a dangerous quest across modern-day America to find the real thief, battle ancient monsters, and prevent a catastrophic war among the gods.
